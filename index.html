How to use this guide

  • Read the brief summary, then click the candidate campaign source.
  • Check whether a candidate has spoken directly about the issue you care about.
  • For incumbents, review official votes, laws, budgets, and outcomes as well as campaign promises.
  • Compare candidates for the same office; powers differ between federal, state, and county roles.

The Ballot Brief says local district, city, and school-board contests can depend on exact street address; ZIP code alone may not show your complete ballot.
